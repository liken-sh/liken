# A series has no progress of its own

Open design problem. A person can mark a film watched or clear its
progress from the film's page, and can do the same for one episode from
that episode's row on the series page (plan 72). Nothing does the same
for a series as a whole.

People watch a series much as they watch one long work: they move
through it in order, stop somewhere in the middle, and come back to
where they stopped. The browser already reads a series that way for
Continue watching, where the thread rule offers the next episode after
a finished one. But the controls a person has are for one episode at a
time, and a series has no page for each episode, nor should it.

So a person cannot:

- clear a whole series, to take it off Continue watching or to start it
  again from the first episode;
- say "watched up to here", to mark every episode before a chosen one
  as watched, for example after watching the first seasons somewhere
  else;
- see the series' place in one view, such as how far through it the
  audience is.

Marking episodes one at a time is the only way to do any of these
today, and for a long series that is not a real option.

The interaction for a series should follow how people watch a series,
as one long work, and not as a list of separate episodes.
