# The home page

Plan 26. A stub for a later agent to shape. It makes the first screen a
home page that blends every kind, instead of a list of libraries.

## The problem

The browser opens on a list of libraries. That was the right first
screen for one movies library and one series library. It is the wrong
one for a house: a person who sits down wants what they were watching,
what arrived this week, the set they are halfway through, and a way
into any library, on one screen. Every library the screen shows is a
question the open problem on which libraries a screen shows still owes
an answer to.

## The shape

- The home page is rows, each one a strip of plan 22's kind with a
  heading: continue watching once plan 14 records positions, recently
  added per library, series with a new episode, sets and franchises,
  and one row of the libraries themselves as the way into a wall.
- Every row is a catalog read the screen already knows how to make,
  ordered by the columns the item header carries, so the home page adds
  queries and no scanner work.
- The band across the top is the same band the walls carry, so sort,
  filter, and search have one home on every screen.
- The home page is what the shade lifts to. Back from a wall returns to
  it, and back from it asks for the shade, as the libraries list does
  today.

## What is not decided

Which rows a screen shows and in what order, and whether that is a
fact of the `Player`, of the room, or of the person. How a row that
has nothing to show, such as continue watching on a fresh cluster,
leaves the page. Whether music and photos get rows here before they
get screens of their own.
