# Larger type on banners and headings

Plan 48. At a 4K panel across a living room, the banners, the
headings, and the franchise cards have room for larger type. The
poster captions on walls and in search results keep their size.

## The problem

Plan 45 put the browser on a 4K panel at scale 2, and the layout is
the 1080p layout. The wall captions read well at that size, because
a wall is dense on purpose. The banners at the top of a page, the
headings on the home page, and the franchise cards are not dense, and
from the sofa their type is smaller than the room can afford.

## The contract

* The banner title and subtitle, the page headings, the genre strip
  headings, and the franchise card titles grow by one step on the
  brand's type scale.
* Poster captions on walls, search results, the continue row, and
  every other two-line card keep their size and their spelling rules.
* The step is one number in the brand theme, so every site and screen
  that takes the theme moves together.

## The proof

A local UI round with the frames captured, then a look on the living
room. This plan waits for that round, because type is judged by eye
on the panel it is for.
