# Concepts

These pages explain the two resources, `Target` and `Solver`, and what
the operator does with them during a night: what a `Target` declares,
how a reservation images it, how its frames move to the archive, and
how a `Solver` plate-solves a frame. The guides give the steps for
each task, and these pages give the reasons behind them.
