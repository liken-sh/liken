# `liken`

`KindKnowledge` resources for the `liken` `Machine` and for the kinds of the media, bluetooth, and observatory operators. `DeviceKnowledge` resources cover the `liken` DRA drivers: the `liken` OS's own driver and the audio, display, bluetooth, and media drivers.
With them, the page of a machine shows the `Machine` under its `Node`, with its hardware titled and described. The page of a `Player` names its screen, speakers, receiver, and remotes, and idle equipment is drawn gray.
