# `liken`

Kind knowledge for `liken`'s `Machine` and for the media, bluetooth, and observatory operators' kinds, and device knowledge for `liken`'s DRA drivers: its own, audio, display, bluetooth, and media.
With it, a machine's page shows the `Machine` underneath its `Node` and its hardware titled and described, a `Player`'s board names its screen, speakers, receiver, and remotes, and idle equipment draws gray.
