# `liken`

`KindKnowledge` resources for the `liken` `Machine` and for the kinds of the bluetooth and observatory operators. `DeviceKnowledge` resources cover the `liken` DRA drivers: the `liken` OS's own driver and the audio, display, bluetooth, and media drivers.
With them, the page of a machine shows the `Machine` under its `Node`, with its hardware titled and described. Idle equipment is drawn gray. The knowledge of the kinds of `media-operator` is in `media-operator/transect/`.
