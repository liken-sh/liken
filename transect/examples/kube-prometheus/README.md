# kube-prometheus

A `MetricsSource` for kube-prometheus's Prometheus and Alertmanager, its cAdvisor, kubelet, and kube-state-metrics series, and its alert rules' labels.
With it, the machines band and the pod bars show usage, a volume's page shows how full it is, "Now" lists the firing alerts on what they are about, and a loss names the alerting path.
