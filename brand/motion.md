# Motion

These rules govern the `liken` mark in motion, wherever a surface
animates it: an idle screen, a site, an installer. The mark is the
fourteen-hexagon mosaic in `liken.svg`, and its motion is one loop
with one control.

## The loop

Each hexagon pulses in size about its own center, up to ten percent
at full energy. Each hexagon uses its own phase and rate, so the
fourteen tiles move independently. The independent pulses are intended
to give the mark an organic appearance. The offsets derive from each
hexagon's index, not from a random source, so the same moment renders
the same way on every run and on every surface.

## The energy

One value, called energy, ranges from zero to one and controls the
loop. At zero the mark is still. This is its resting state; do not
animate it for decoration. Energy controls both the size of each
pulse and its speed. As energy decreases, the pulses become smaller
and slower. Ease every change in energy rather than changing it
abruptly.

## What motion means

The mark moves only while the system performs work that a person
waits for. Ramp the energy up as the work starts, hold it while the
work runs in view, and ease it back to zero as the work ends. Keep the
mark still at rest, so motion indicates work in progress.
