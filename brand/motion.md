# Motion

These rules govern the `liken` mark in motion, wherever a surface
animates it: an idle screen, a site, an installer. The mark is the
fourteen-hexagon mosaic in `liken.svg`, and its motion is one loop
with one control.

## The loop

Each hexagon pulses in size about its own center, up to ten percent
at full energy. Each hexagon takes its own phase and its own rate, so
the fourteen move independently and the whole reads as one organic
thing. The offsets derive from each hexagon's index, not from a
random source, so the same moment renders the same way on every run
and on every surface.

## The energy

One scalar from zero to one drives the loop. At zero the mark is
still, and a still mark is the resting state: the mark does not move
to decorate. Energy scales the swing and the speed together, so a
mark losing energy both shrinks its motion and slows it. Ease every
change of energy; never step it.

## What motion means

The mark moves only while the system works on something a person
waits for. Ramp the energy up as the work starts, hold it while the
work runs in view, and ease it back to zero as the work ends. Motion
at rest is ruled out, because a viewer must be able to read motion
as work in progress.
